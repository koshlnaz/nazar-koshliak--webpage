03 — How I work

The deal process.

Six steps from first call to keys in your hand. No black boxes — every stage in writing.

01

Discovery

30-min call. Goals, horizon, ticket size, family situation, tax status. I listen — no pitch.

~ 30 minutes
02

Written brief

Within 72 hours: market positioning, comparable transactions, holding-cost model, exit window, risk flags.

~ 72 hours
03

Shortlist

3 — 5 units that fit the thesis. Not a hundred listings to overwhelm — a handful that actually match.

~ 1 week
04

Site visits

I walk the streets, the lobbies, the model floors with you. We see what the photos don't show.

~ 1 — 2 days
05

Negotiation & SPA

Offer, counter, payment plan, contract review. I sit on your side of the table and the developer's calendar.

~ 1 — 3 weeks
06

Handover & beyond

Snagging, lease structuring, tenant placement, quarterly performance review. The relationship doesn't end at handover.
