Chapter 2 · Yours

Yours.

06 · Ownership

40% of residential value is foreign-owned.

Most of Dubai is open to foreigners — and most buyers already are. 100% ownership in your name (or company). No local partner. No residency required. Every title deed is registered with the Dubai Land Department.

Sources: International Tax Observatory 2024 · Dubai Land Department · ValuStrat 2025.
07 · Alternatives

It's not even comparable.

Dubai is the only major property market with no ownership restrictions, no taxes, and a 10-year visa attached. Bali yields look highest until you read the fine print: no freehold, nominee structures, currency risk. Thailand caps foreign ownership at 49%. Georgia is open but the market is 100× smaller than Dubai.

Sources: Housebook MENA 2025 · ValuStrat · Tekce 2026.
08 · Buyer protection

Your money sits in escrow. Not the developer's pocket.

Every off-plan purchase is protected by Dubai Land Department law. This is the strictest off-plan buyer-protection framework in the GCC — and stronger than most European markets.

Sources: RERA · Dubai Land Department · Engel & Völkers Escrow Guide 2026.
09 · Residency

Your property buys you residency. Two options.

Buy a property. Get a visa. For you and your family. Updated April 2026.

Standard pathway

2 years, no minimum value

Any sole owner of a completed residential unit qualifies — regardless of price.

Joint owners: AED 400,000 individual share minimum.

Renewable. Sponsors family.

Premium pathway · Golden Visa

10 years

AED 2,000,000+ property (full value, not down payment).

Multiple properties can combine.

Off-plan now eligible (Feb 2026 — no 50% upfront).

Sponsors spouse, children, parents, household staff.

No minimum stay required.

Sources: Dubai Land Department · VisaHQ 2026 · Gulf News Dubai Visa Guide 2026.
